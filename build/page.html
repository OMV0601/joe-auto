    Before you ring

    Four things worth having ready.

    The more of this he has up front, the closer his first answer gets to a real number.

    1. 01

      The car

      Year, make, model. Engine size too, if you know it — parts change between trims.

    2. 02

      What it’s actually doing

      Sounds, smells, warning lights, when it started. “Squeals on cold starts” beats “making a noise.”

    3. 03

      Where it’s parked

      He’s coming to the car, so it matters whether it’s a driveway, a garage, or a kerb on a hill.

    4. 04

      Whether it still drives

      A car that won’t start is a different visit from one that just needs an oil change.
